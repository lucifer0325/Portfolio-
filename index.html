02 PHILOSOPHY

THE ALGORITHM DOESN'T DECIDE RESULTS.
YOUR INPUTS DO.

Most marketers blame the algorithm. I blame the inputs. Weak offer. Boring hook. Messy signals. Funnel leaks. I fix the inputs — so the algorithm works for me, not against me.

01

Weak Offer

No targeting can sell something people don't want. The offer must create extreme value.

02

Boring Hook

If your ad doesn't earn attention in 2 seconds, the algorithm can't save it. Hooks are the targeting.

03

Messy Signals

Poor event setup, noisy data, and wrong optimization goals confuse the system. Clean CAPI = predictive power.

04

Impatience

Killing ads during learning phase ≠ smart optimization. Let the pixel breathe and optimize based on maturity.

05

Funnel Leaks

Ads don't convert when landing pages are slow, unclear, or misaligned. The page completes what the ad promised.

ETHNIC BOUTIQUE — THE SAREE STORY
Fashion D2C

Problem: Polished studio shoots looked premium but felt distant. CAC ₹1,500+. Revenue stagnant at ₹5L on ₹2L spend.

Insight: A saree is not a product. It's an emotion. The woman scrolling needs to see herself in it.

System Architecture:
  • Killed studio shoots. Went outdoor: villa, lake, garden, natural light, trending audio.
  • Aggressive carousel testing — let data pick the winner, not gut.
  • Added saree name, color, and price directly on static creatives. Removed friction.
  • Tightened audience: 25+ female, top tier cities only, one high-intent city exclusively.

05 METHODOLOGY

IF YOUR ADS AREN'T WORKING, STOP BLAMING THE ALGORITHM.

Here is what actually breaks performance — in order:

1

Weak Offer

No amount of targeting can sell something people don't want. The foundation must be valuable.

2

Boring Hook

If your ad doesn't earn attention in 2 seconds, the algorithm can't save it. Attention is currency.

3

Messy Signals

Poor event setup, noisy data, or wrong optimization goals confuse the system. Clean tracking is required.

4

Impatience

Killing ads during the learning phase ≠ smart optimization. Data accumulation takes time.

5

Funnel Leaks

Ads don't convert when landing pages are slow, unclear, or misaligned. Optimize the destination.

"Strong inputs = freedom. Weak inputs = excuses. High-performing marketers focus less on dashboards and more on offers, creatives, and systems."
